### My Favorite Series: "Baki Hanma"

"Baki Hanma" is a Japanese manga series written and illustrated by Keisuke Itagaki. It follows Baki Hanma as he trains and tests his fighting skills against various opponents in deadly, no-rules hand-to-hand combat.

*Main Characters:*

-   **Baki Hanma:** The protagonist, driven to surpass his father.
-   **Yujiro Hanma:** Baki's incredibly strong and ruthless father, known as the "Ogre."

> "A relentless pursuit of strength and the ultimate battle."

*Why I Love It:*
1. The intense and over-the-top fight scenes.
2. The unique and diverse cast of characters.
3. The constant focus on self-improvement and pushing one's limits.
